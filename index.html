About The CEO

Jerry Frankline Yudaha ("Otiepe") | CEO & Founder

Greetings! I am Jerry Frankline Yudaha, affectionately known by my moniker "Otiepe". As a passionate full-stack developer, graphic designer, video editor, and tech entrepreneur, I am dedicated to bridging the gap between imaginative concepts and functional technology. I serve as the Chief Executive Officer of JFY MULTIPURPOSE TECHNOLOGIES—a dynamic technological venture driven by logical engineering and visual artistry.

My philosophy centers around multi-purpose innovation. Technology should not be rigid; it must adapt, solve real-world problems, and deliver impactful visual storytelling. Over the years, I have spearheaded creative campaigns, developed digital applications, designed custom identity credentials, and provided technical consultation to individuals, educational bodies, and political entities.

My foundational experiences include serving as the Technical Manager, Graphic Designer, and Web Editor for the Achungo Journalism Club (AJCTV). Through this role, I designed high-profile broadcast overlays, digital staff ID badges, news tickers, and official publication art that significantly elevated the brand identity of the club and institution.

My STEM Journey & Technological Foundations

My passion for technology began with algorithmic block-based programming on Scratch, where I learned to structure logic and visualize code execution. Seeking deeper technical mastery, I completed structured frontend web development training at Codingal, while expanding my knowledge through self-guided study across platforms like W3Schools, freeCodeCamp, and MDN Web Docs.

Today, my skill set encompasses HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), Python, and modern web application development. Furthermore, my strong background in Mathematics and Integrated Science equips me with analytical problem-solving skills, allowing me to approach code architecture, system debugging, and layout design with precision and creativity.
